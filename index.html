Shuju Shi

Welcome!

I am an Assistant Professor in Computational Linguistics at Indiana University Bloomington, with adjunct appointments at Computer Science and Cognitive Science.

My research uses speech technology and acoustic phonetics to understand how people produce and perceive speech, especially atypical speech such as non-native and dysarthric speech, and to build tools that help speakers communicate better.

I received my Ph.D. from the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign in 2022 under the supervision of Chilin Shih, Mark Hasegawa-Johnson, and Ryan Shosted.
